# Impeccable is the primary workflow; Taste is a gate for marketing sites

Impeccable and Taste both prescribe how a frontend should look and will sometimes
disagree. Impeccable wins by default: it covers app UI, dashboards and design
systems end to end. Taste scopes itself to company websites, so it is applied only
there, as an extra pass/fail bar on top of Impeccable rather than a competing
workflow.
